## What it adds

Break one ore and every connected ore of the same kind breaks with it.

- Max Blocks: how many blocks one vein may have (1 to 64)
- Silk Touch: follow your tool, always or never
- Durability Cost: tool damage per extra block
- Whitelist: the ores that count as a vein
